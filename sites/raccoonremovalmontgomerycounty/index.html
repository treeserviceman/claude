Why It Happens

Raccoon Removal in Montgomery County

A raccoon in the attic is more than a nuisance. Their droppings can carry roundworm and other parasites, their claws tear ductwork and insulation, and a female raccoon will happily raise a litter of kits in your soffit if given the chance. We’ve built our Montgomery County raccoon removal process around solving the problem completely: inspect, remove, exclude, repair.

Homes and businesses across Montgomery County deal with seasonal weather swings that push wildlife to seek shelter indoors — exactly the conditions that send raccoons looking for a warm, dry attic to den in.

Signs You Have Raccoons

  • 1
    Raccoons seen at dusk near the roofline

    Repeated sightings of a raccoon climbing your home in the same spot usually means it already has a way inside.

  • 2
    Visible entry or exit holes

    Chewed fascia boards, pulled-back flashing, or a gap at the chimney cap are all common raccoon access points.

  • 3
    Torn soffits, vents, or roof edges

    Raccoons use their strength and dexterity to rip open weak points around rooflines and gable vents to get inside.

  • 4
    Heavy footsteps or scratching overhead

    Raccoons are large and noisy compared to squirrels or mice — you’ll usually hear movement in the evening and early morning.

  • 5
    Flattened insulation or stained ceiling spots

    Denning raccoons compress insulation and their waste can eventually stain drywall or ceiling tile.

  • 6
    A strong, musky odor

    Urine and droppings build up quickly in a den site and produce a distinct, unpleasant smell you can often notice from inside the home.
