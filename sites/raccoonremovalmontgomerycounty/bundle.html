Signs You Have Raccoons

  • 1
    Visible nesting material pulled through vents

    Shredded insulation, leaves, or debris protruding from a vent or gap is a sign a den is already established.

  • 2
    Visible entry or exit holes

    Chewed fascia boards, pulled-back flashing, or a gap at the chimney cap are all common raccoon access points.

  • 3
    Grease marks or smudges around roof entry points

    Raccoons squeezing through tight gaps repeatedly leave dark, oily smudges around the edges of the opening.

  • 4
    Water stains that appear without recent rain

    Urine saturation in insulation can mimic a roof leak, showing up as a ceiling stain with no storm to explain it.

  • 5
    A sagging or stained section of ceiling drywall

    Urine soaking into insulation over time can eventually seep through drywall, leaving a visible stain.

  • 6
    Rustling sounds after dark

    Raccoons are nocturnal, so most activity — and most noise — happens once the house has gone quiet for the night.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does an inspection actually involve?

We check the roofline, soffits, vents, chimney, and foundation for entry points and signs of activity, then walk you through exactly what we found before any work begins.

What smell will I notice, and does it go away?

A musky, ammonia-like odor from urine and droppings is common with an active den. It fades once contaminated insulation is removed and the area is sanitized.

How much does raccoon removal cost in Montgomery County?

Cost depends on the number of entry points, whether there’s a litter involved, and how much attic repair is needed. We provide a clear, written estimate after a full inspection — call (833) 901-3895 for a quote.

Will the raccoon come back after removal?

Not if the entry point is properly sealed. That’s why exclusion — not just trapping — is the core of our process; a trapped raccoon with an open entry point just gets replaced by the next one.

How long does the process take?

A single adult raccoon can often be excluded within a few days. If a litter is denning in the attic, we wait for the kits to be mobile before completing exclusion, which can take one to a few weeks.

Do you serve businesses as well as homes?

Yes. We handle raccoon removal and exclusion for warehouses, restaurants, retail buildings, and multi-family properties in and around Montgomery County.
