Common Warning Signs

  • 1
    Torn soffits, vents, or roof edges

    Raccoons use their strength and dexterity to rip open weak points around rooflines and gable vents to get inside.

  • 2
    A raccoon seen carrying food toward the roofline

    A raccoon repeatedly hauling food up toward the same spot on the roof is very likely feeding kits in an active den.

  • 3
    Visible entry or exit holes

    Chewed fascia boards, pulled-back flashing, or a gap at the chimney cap are all common raccoon access points.

  • 4
    Rustling sounds after dark

    Raccoons are nocturnal, so most activity — and most noise — happens once the house has gone quiet for the night.

  • 5
    Paw prints in dust, snow, or mud near the roofline

    Distinct five-toed tracks around downspouts, decks, or roof access points are a strong indicator of raccoon traffic.

  • 6
    A sagging or stained section of ceiling drywall

    Urine soaking into insulation over time can eventually seep through drywall, leaving a visible stain.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does raccoon removal cost in Denton, TX?

Cost depends on the number of entry points, whether there’s a litter involved, and how much attic repair is needed. We provide a clear, written estimate after a full inspection — call (833) 901-3895 for a quote.

Can I just seal the entry point myself?

Not until the raccoon is confirmed out — sealing an active den traps the animal inside, which usually leads to a much worse problem (and a bigger odor issue) than the original one.

What if there’s a litter of kits in the attic?

We locate the den, confirm the kits are mobile, and use a one-way door timed so the mother can move them out on her own before we seal the entry point.

Do you repair the damage afterward?

Yes. We offer attic insulation removal and replacement, sanitation, and structural repair for soffits, vents, and fascia after the raccoons are safely excluded.

Do you use poison?

No. Poison is not humane, not legal for wildlife control in most jurisdictions, and risks a raccoon dying inside a wall or attic cavity — we don’t use it.

Do you serve businesses as well as homes?

Yes. We handle raccoon removal and exclusion for warehouses, restaurants, retail buildings, and multi-family properties in and around Denton, TX.
