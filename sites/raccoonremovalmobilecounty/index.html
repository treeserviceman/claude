Raccoon Removal in Mobile County

A raccoon in an attic in Mobile County isn’t a one-time visitor — once it finds a warm, dry spot, it settles in. A single entry point rarely stays single for long — raccoons will widen a gap or open a second one nearby once they’re established. Our licensed Mobile County technicians document what they find, walk you through the plan, and follow through until it’s fixed.

Homes and businesses across Mobile County deal with seasonal weather swings that push wildlife to seek shelter indoors — exactly the conditions that send raccoons looking for a warm, dry attic to den in.

Signs You Have Raccoons

  • 1
    A strong, musky odor

    Urine and droppings build up quickly in a den site and produce a distinct, unpleasant smell you can often notice from inside the home.

  • 2
    Trash cans knocked over or raided repeatedly

    Raccoons denning nearby will often forage close to home, and unsecured trash is an easy target.

  • 3
    Torn soffits, vents, or roof edges

    Raccoons use their strength and dexterity to rip open weak points around rooflines and gable vents to get inside.

  • 4
    Rustling sounds after dark

    Raccoons are nocturnal, so most activity — and most noise — happens once the house has gone quiet for the night.

  • 5
    Visible entry or exit holes

    Chewed fascia boards, pulled-back flashing, or a gap at the chimney cap are all common raccoon access points.

  • 6
    A sagging or stained section of ceiling drywall

    Urine soaking into insulation over time can eventually seep through drywall, leaving a visible stain.
