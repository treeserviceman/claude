Signs You Have Raccoons

  • 1
    Torn soffits, vents, or roof edges

    Raccoons use their strength and dexterity to rip open weak points around rooflines and gable vents to get inside.

  • 2
    Visible entry or exit holes

    Chewed fascia boards, pulled-back flashing, or a gap at the chimney cap are all common raccoon access points.

  • 3
    Paw prints in dust, snow, or mud near the roofline

    Distinct five-toed tracks around downspouts, decks, or roof access points are a strong indicator of raccoon traffic.

  • 4
    Rustling sounds after dark

    Raccoons are nocturnal, so most activity — and most noise — happens once the house has gone quiet for the night.

  • 5
    Grease marks or smudges around roof entry points

    Raccoons squeezing through tight gaps repeatedly leave dark, oily smudges around the edges of the opening.

  • 6
    A strong, musky odor

    Urine and droppings build up quickly in a den site and produce a distinct, unpleasant smell you can often notice from inside the home.

Common Questions

Raccoon Removal: Common Questions

How long does the process take?

A single adult raccoon can often be excluded within a few days. If a litter is denning in the attic, we wait for the kits to be mobile before completing exclusion, which can take one to a few weeks.

Do you repair the damage afterward?

Yes. We offer attic insulation removal and replacement, sanitation, and structural repair for soffits, vents, and fascia after the raccoons are safely excluded.

What does an inspection actually involve?

We check the roofline, soffits, vents, chimney, and foundation for entry points and signs of activity, then walk you through exactly what we found before any work begins.

What smell will I notice, and does it go away?

A musky, ammonia-like odor from urine and droppings is common with an active den. It fades once contaminated insulation is removed and the area is sanitized.

Can I just seal the entry point myself?

Not until the raccoon is confirmed out — sealing an active den traps the animal inside, which usually leads to a much worse problem (and a bigger odor issue) than the original one.

Do you serve businesses as well as homes?

Yes. We handle raccoon removal and exclusion for warehouses, restaurants, retail buildings, and multi-family properties in and around Dallas County.
