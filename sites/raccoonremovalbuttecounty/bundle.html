The Root Cause

Butte County Raccoon Problems, Solved

Attics in Butte County make ideal raccoon dens: dark, insulated, elevated, and usually quiet during the day. Once one gets into an attic, it rarely stays alone for long — mothers den up with litters, and what starts as a scratching noise at night can turn into torn insulation, chewed wiring, and a lingering odor problem within weeks. We’ve built our Butte County raccoon removal process around solving the problem completely: inspect, remove, exclude, repair.

Homes and businesses across Butte County deal with seasonal weather swings that push wildlife to seek shelter indoors — exactly the conditions that send raccoons looking for a warm, dry attic to den in.

Common Warning Signs

  • 1
    New gaps appearing around existing roof repairs

    A raccoon that finds a previously repaired weak spot will often reopen it rather than search for a brand-new one.

  • 2
    A sagging or stained section of ceiling drywall

    Urine soaking into insulation over time can eventually seep through drywall, leaving a visible stain.

  • 3
    Trash cans knocked over or raided repeatedly

    Raccoons denning nearby will often forage close to home, and unsecured trash is an easy target.

  • 4
    Unusual pet behavior near a wall or vent

    Dogs and cats will often bark, whine, or stare fixedly at a wall, ceiling, or vent where wildlife is denning nearby.

  • 5
    Water stains that appear without recent rain

    Urine saturation in insulation can mimic a roof leak, showing up as a ceiling stain with no storm to explain it.

  • 6
    Visible nesting material pulled through vents

    Shredded insulation, leaves, or debris protruding from a vent or gap is a sign a den is already established.

Raccoon Removal: Common Questions

How much does raccoon removal cost in Butte County?

Cost depends on the number of entry points, whether there’s a litter involved, and how much attic repair is needed. We provide a clear, written estimate after a full inspection — call (833) 901-3895 for a quote.

Can I just seal the entry point myself?

Not until the raccoon is confirmed out — sealing an active den traps the animal inside, which usually leads to a much worse problem (and a bigger odor issue) than the original one.

How long does the process take?

A single adult raccoon can often be excluded within a few days. If a litter is denning in the attic, we wait for the kits to be mobile before completing exclusion, which can take one to a few weeks.

Do you use poison?

No. Poison is not humane, not legal for wildlife control in most jurisdictions, and risks a raccoon dying inside a wall or attic cavity — we don’t use it.

Do you serve businesses as well as homes?

Yes. We handle raccoon removal and exclusion for warehouses, restaurants, retail buildings, and multi-family properties in and around Butte County.
