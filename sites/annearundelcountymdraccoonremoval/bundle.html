Signs You Have Raccoons

  • 1
    Torn soffits, vents, or roof edges

    Raccoons use their strength and dexterity to rip open weak points around rooflines and gable vents to get inside.

  • 2
    New gaps appearing around existing roof repairs

    A raccoon that finds a previously repaired weak spot will often reopen it rather than search for a brand-new one.

  • 3
    Visible entry or exit holes

    Chewed fascia boards, pulled-back flashing, or a gap at the chimney cap are all common raccoon access points.

  • 4
    Raccoons seen at dusk near the roofline

    Repeated sightings of a raccoon climbing your home in the same spot usually means it already has a way inside.

  • 5
    Water stains that appear without recent rain

    Urine saturation in insulation can mimic a roof leak, showing up as a ceiling stain with no storm to explain it.

  • 6
    Trash cans knocked over or raided repeatedly

    Raccoons denning nearby will often forage close to home, and unsecured trash is an easy target.

Common Questions

Raccoon Removal FAQ

What smell will I notice, and does it go away?

A musky, ammonia-like odor from urine and droppings is common with an active den. It fades once contaminated insulation is removed and the area is sanitized.

Do you use poison?

No. Poison is not humane, not legal for wildlife control in most jurisdictions, and risks a raccoon dying inside a wall or attic cavity — we don’t use it.

What does an inspection actually involve?

We check the roofline, soffits, vents, chimney, and foundation for entry points and signs of activity, then walk you through exactly what we found before any work begins.

Do you serve businesses as well as homes?

Yes. We handle raccoon removal and exclusion for warehouses, restaurants, retail buildings, and multi-family properties in and around Anne Arundel County, MD.

Are raccoons dangerous?

Raccoons can carry rabies and their droppings can contain roundworm eggs, so direct contact should always be avoided. Our technicians handle wildlife and cleanup safely so you don’t have to.

Do you repair the damage afterward?

Yes. We offer attic insulation removal and replacement, sanitation, and structural repair for soffits, vents, and fascia after the raccoons are safely excluded.
