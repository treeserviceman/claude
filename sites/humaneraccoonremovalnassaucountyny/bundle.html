Signs You Have Raccoons

  • 1
    Trash cans knocked over or raided repeatedly

    Raccoons denning nearby will often forage close to home, and unsecured trash is an easy target.

  • 2
    A strong, musky odor

    Urine and droppings build up quickly in a den site and produce a distinct, unpleasant smell you can often notice from inside the home.

  • 3
    Paw prints in dust, snow, or mud near the roofline

    Distinct five-toed tracks around downspouts, decks, or roof access points are a strong indicator of raccoon traffic.

  • 4
    Rustling sounds after dark

    Raccoons are nocturnal, so most activity — and most noise — happens once the house has gone quiet for the night.

  • 5
    Water stains that appear without recent rain

    Urine saturation in insulation can mimic a roof leak, showing up as a ceiling stain with no storm to explain it.

  • 6
    Grease marks or smudges around roof entry points

    Raccoons squeezing through tight gaps repeatedly leave dark, oily smudges around the edges of the opening.

Raccoons can carry rabies and their droppings can contain roundworm eggs, so direct contact should always be avoided. Our technicians handle wildlife and cleanup safely so you don’t have to.
