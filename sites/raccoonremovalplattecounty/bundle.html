Why Platte County Homes Get Raccoons

Raccoons in Platte County are opportunistic denners, and an attic offers exactly what they’re looking for: warmth, height, and safety from predators. What starts as occasional noise at dusk can escalate quickly into torn vents, stained ceilings, and a den full of kits by spring. We’ve built our Platte County raccoon removal process around solving the problem completely: inspect, remove, exclude, repair.

How to Tell You Have Raccoons

  • 1
    Raccoons seen at dusk near the roofline

    Repeated sightings of a raccoon climbing your home in the same spot usually means it already has a way inside.

  • 2
    Trash cans knocked over or raided repeatedly

    Raccoons denning nearby will often forage close to home, and unsecured trash is an easy target.

  • 3
    Chewed or displaced roof vent covers

    Raccoons will pry loose or chew through plastic and metal vent covers to widen an opening into the attic.

  • 4
    Unusual pet behavior near a wall or vent

    Dogs and cats will often bark, whine, or stare fixedly at a wall, ceiling, or vent where wildlife is denning nearby.

  • 5
    Loud thumping or rolling sounds during the day

    A litter of kits moving around while the mother is out foraging often sounds like rolling or thumping overhead.

  • 6
    A strong, musky odor

    Urine and droppings build up quickly in a den site and produce a distinct, unpleasant smell you can often notice from inside the home.

Platte County Raccoon Removal FAQ

How much does raccoon removal cost in Platte County?

Cost depends on the number of entry points, whether there’s a litter involved, and how much attic repair is needed. We provide a clear, written estimate after a full inspection — call (833) 901-3895 for a quote.

Do you use poison?

No. Poison is not humane, not legal for wildlife control in most jurisdictions, and risks a raccoon dying inside a wall or attic cavity — we don’t use it.

Do you offer emergency service?

Yes, we prioritize active intrusions and exposed entry points with same-day or next-day emergency appointments where available.

Are raccoons dangerous?

Raccoons can carry rabies and their droppings can contain roundworm eggs, so direct contact should always be avoided. Our technicians handle wildlife and cleanup safely so you don’t have to.

What smell will I notice, and does it go away?

A musky, ammonia-like odor from urine and droppings is common with an active den. It fades once contaminated insulation is removed and the area is sanitized.

Do you repair the damage afterward?

Yes. We offer attic insulation removal and replacement, sanitation, and structural repair for soffits, vents, and fascia after the raccoons are safely excluded.
