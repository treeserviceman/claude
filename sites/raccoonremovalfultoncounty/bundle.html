Raccoons in Fulton County: What to Know

Attics in Fulton County make ideal raccoon dens: dark, insulated, elevated, and usually quiet during the day. Once one gets into an attic, it rarely stays alone for long — mothers den up with litters, and what starts as a scratching noise at night can turn into torn insulation, chewed wiring, and a lingering odor problem within weeks. We’ve built our Fulton County raccoon removal process around solving the problem completely: inspect, remove, exclude, repair.

Homes and businesses across Fulton County deal with seasonal weather swings that push wildlife to seek shelter indoors — exactly the conditions that send raccoons looking for a warm, dry attic to den in.

Common Warning Signs

  • 1
    Visible nesting material pulled through vents

    Shredded insulation, leaves, or debris protruding from a vent or gap is a sign a den is already established.

  • 2
    Damaged chimney caps or spark arrestors

    A bent, missing, or pried-open chimney cap is one of the most common raccoon access points in older homes.

  • 3
    Loud thumping or rolling sounds during the day

    A litter of kits moving around while the mother is out foraging often sounds like rolling or thumping overhead.

  • 4
    Raccoons seen at dusk near the roofline

    Repeated sightings of a raccoon climbing your home in the same spot usually means it already has a way inside.

  • 5
    Rustling sounds after dark

    Raccoons are nocturnal, so most activity — and most noise — happens once the house has gone quiet for the night.

  • 6
    Grease marks or smudges around roof entry points

    Raccoons squeezing through tight gaps repeatedly leave dark, oily smudges around the edges of the opening.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does raccoon removal cost in Fulton County?

Cost depends on the number of entry points, whether there’s a litter involved, and how much attic repair is needed. We provide a clear, written estimate after a full inspection — call (833) 901-3895 for a quote.

Are raccoons dangerous?

Raccoons can carry rabies and their droppings can contain roundworm eggs, so direct contact should always be avoided. Our technicians handle wildlife and cleanup safely so you don’t have to.

What if there’s a litter of kits in the attic?

We locate the den, confirm the kits are mobile, and use a one-way door timed so the mother can move them out on her own before we seal the entry point.

Do you repair the damage afterward?

Yes. We offer attic insulation removal and replacement, sanitation, and structural repair for soffits, vents, and fascia after the raccoons are safely excluded.

Do you serve businesses as well as homes?

Yes. We handle raccoon removal and exclusion for warehouses, restaurants, retail buildings, and multi-family properties in and around Fulton County.

How long does the process take?

A single adult raccoon can often be excluded within a few days. If a litter is denning in the attic, we wait for the kits to be mobile before completing exclusion, which can take one to a few weeks.
