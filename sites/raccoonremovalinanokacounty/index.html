Understanding the Problem

Understanding Raccoon Activity in Anoka County

In Anoka County, raccoons commonly target attics, chimneys, and crawl spaces because they mimic the hollow trees raccoons naturally den in. Beyond the noise, raccoons chew wiring and ductwork, which creates real fire and HVAC risks if the problem sits too long. Our team handles raccoon removal throughout Anoka County using humane, code-compliant methods — not poison, not guesswork.

Homes and businesses across Anoka County deal with seasonal weather swings that push wildlife to seek shelter indoors — exactly the conditions that send raccoons looking for a warm, dry attic to den in.

Signs of a Raccoon Problem

  • 1
    Loud thumping or rolling sounds during the day

    A litter of kits moving around while the mother is out foraging often sounds like rolling or thumping overhead.

  • 2
    Trash cans knocked over or raided repeatedly

    Raccoons denning nearby will often forage close to home, and unsecured trash is an easy target.

  • 3
    Damaged chimney caps or spark arrestors

    A bent, missing, or pried-open chimney cap is one of the most common raccoon access points in older homes.

  • 4
    A sagging or stained section of ceiling drywall

    Urine soaking into insulation over time can eventually seep through drywall, leaving a visible stain.

  • 5
    A raccoon seen carrying food toward the roofline

    A raccoon repeatedly hauling food up toward the same spot on the roof is very likely feeding kits in an active den.

  • 6
    Paw prints in dust, snow, or mud near the roofline

    Distinct five-toed tracks around downspouts, decks, or roof access points are a strong indicator of raccoon traffic.
