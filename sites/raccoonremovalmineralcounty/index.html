Is It Raccoons? Look For This

  • 1
    Trash cans knocked over or raided repeatedly

    Raccoons denning nearby will often forage close to home, and unsecured trash is an easy target.

  • 2
    Visible entry or exit holes

    Chewed fascia boards, pulled-back flashing, or a gap at the chimney cap are all common raccoon access points.

  • 3
    Visible nesting material pulled through vents

    Shredded insulation, leaves, or debris protruding from a vent or gap is a sign a den is already established.

  • 4
    Rustling sounds after dark

    Raccoons are nocturnal, so most activity — and most noise — happens once the house has gone quiet for the night.

  • 5
    Loud thumping or rolling sounds during the day

    A litter of kits moving around while the mother is out foraging often sounds like rolling or thumping overhead.

  • 6
    Grease marks or smudges around roof entry points

    Raccoons squeezing through tight gaps repeatedly leave dark, oily smudges around the edges of the opening.
