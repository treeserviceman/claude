Understanding the Problem

The Richardson, TX Raccoon Problem

Raccoons are smart, strong, and surprisingly good at finding the weakest point in a roofline, soffit, or chimney cap in Richardson, TX. The longer a raccoon dens undisturbed, the more insulation, wiring, and drywall ends up needing repair afterward. Our approach in Richardson, TX focuses on permanent results: seal what let the raccoon in, not just remove the one that’s there now.

Homes and businesses across Richardson, TX deal with hot summers and mild winters that keep raccoons active nearly year-round — exactly the conditions that send raccoons looking for a warm, dry attic to den in.

Signs You Have Raccoons

  • 1
    Grease marks or smudges around roof entry points

    Raccoons squeezing through tight gaps repeatedly leave dark, oily smudges around the edges of the opening.

  • 2
    Raccoons seen at dusk near the roofline

    Repeated sightings of a raccoon climbing your home in the same spot usually means it already has a way inside.

  • 3
    Damaged chimney caps or spark arrestors

    A bent, missing, or pried-open chimney cap is one of the most common raccoon access points in older homes.

  • 4
    Rustling sounds after dark

    Raccoons are nocturnal, so most activity — and most noise — happens once the house has gone quiet for the night.

  • 5
    New gaps appearing around existing roof repairs

    A raccoon that finds a previously repaired weak spot will often reopen it rather than search for a brand-new one.

  • 6
    Unusual pet behavior near a wall or vent

    Dogs and cats will often bark, whine, or stare fixedly at a wall, ceiling, or vent where wildlife is denning nearby.
