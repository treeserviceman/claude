Understanding the Problem

Raccoon Removal in Rice County

It doesn’t take much for a raccoon to get into a home in Rice County — a loose vent screen or a gap under a roof edge is usually all it needs. If you’ve heard heavy footsteps overhead, found displaced insulation, or noticed a strong, musky smell near your attic vents, you likely have raccoons denning on your property. Every Rice County job follows the same standard: locate every entry point, resolve it humanely, then seal it for good.

Homes and businesses across Rice County deal with seasonal weather swings that push wildlife to seek shelter indoors — exactly the conditions that send raccoons looking for a warm, dry attic to den in.

Signs You Have Raccoons

  • 1
    Torn soffits, vents, or roof edges

    Raccoons use their strength and dexterity to rip open weak points around rooflines and gable vents to get inside.

  • 2
    Raccoons seen at dusk near the roofline

    Repeated sightings of a raccoon climbing your home in the same spot usually means it already has a way inside.

  • 3
    Visible entry or exit holes

    Chewed fascia boards, pulled-back flashing, or a gap at the chimney cap are all common raccoon access points.

  • 4
    Paw prints in dust, snow, or mud near the roofline

    Distinct five-toed tracks around downspouts, decks, or roof access points are a strong indicator of raccoon traffic.

  • 5
    A strong, musky odor

    Urine and droppings build up quickly in a den site and produce a distinct, unpleasant smell you can often notice from inside the home.

  • 6
    Trash cans knocked over or raided repeatedly

    Raccoons denning nearby will often forage close to home, and unsecured trash is an easy target.
