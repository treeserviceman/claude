Signs You Have Raccoons

  • 1
    A sagging or stained section of ceiling drywall

    Urine soaking into insulation over time can eventually seep through drywall, leaving a visible stain.

  • 2
    Unusual pet behavior near a wall or vent

    Dogs and cats will often bark, whine, or stare fixedly at a wall, ceiling, or vent where wildlife is denning nearby.

  • 3
    Trash cans knocked over or raided repeatedly

    Raccoons denning nearby will often forage close to home, and unsecured trash is an easy target.

  • 4
    New gaps appearing around existing roof repairs

    A raccoon that finds a previously repaired weak spot will often reopen it rather than search for a brand-new one.

  • 5
    Water stains that appear without recent rain

    Urine saturation in insulation can mimic a roof leak, showing up as a ceiling stain with no storm to explain it.

  • 6
    Rustling sounds after dark

    Raccoons are nocturnal, so most activity — and most noise — happens once the house has gone quiet for the night.

Common Questions

Raccoon Removal FAQ

Can raccoons damage electrical wiring?

Yes, chewed wiring is a common and serious risk with attic denning, which is one more reason to address a raccoon problem quickly rather than wait.

Can I just seal the entry point myself?

Not until the raccoon is confirmed out — sealing an active den traps the animal inside, which usually leads to a much worse problem (and a bigger odor issue) than the original one.

Do you offer emergency service?

Yes, we prioritize active intrusions and exposed entry points with same-day or next-day emergency appointments where available.

Do you serve businesses as well as homes?

Yes. We handle raccoon removal and exclusion for warehouses, restaurants, retail buildings, and multi-family properties in and around Linn County.

What smell will I notice, and does it go away?

A musky, ammonia-like odor from urine and droppings is common with an active den. It fades once contaminated insulation is removed and the area is sanitized.

Is there a warranty on the repair work?

Sealed entry points are backed by a workmanship warranty — if a raccoon reopens the same spot, we return to fix it at no extra charge.
