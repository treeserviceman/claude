Is It Raccoons? Look For This

  • 1
    Grease marks or smudges around roof entry points

    Raccoons squeezing through tight gaps repeatedly leave dark, oily smudges around the edges of the opening.

  • 2
    Raccoons seen at dusk near the roofline

    Repeated sightings of a raccoon climbing your home in the same spot usually means it already has a way inside.

  • 3
    New gaps appearing around existing roof repairs

    A raccoon that finds a previously repaired weak spot will often reopen it rather than search for a brand-new one.

  • 4
    Rustling sounds after dark

    Raccoons are nocturnal, so most activity — and most noise — happens once the house has gone quiet for the night.

  • 5
    Trash cans knocked over or raided repeatedly

    Raccoons denning nearby will often forage close to home, and unsecured trash is an easy target.

  • 6
    A strong, musky odor

    Urine and droppings build up quickly in a den site and produce a distinct, unpleasant smell you can often notice from inside the home.

Butler County Raccoon Removal FAQ

How much does raccoon removal cost in Butler County?

Cost depends on the number of entry points, whether there’s a litter involved, and how much attic repair is needed. We provide a clear, written estimate after a full inspection — call (833) 901-3895 for a quote.

Can I just seal the entry point myself?

Not until the raccoon is confirmed out — sealing an active den traps the animal inside, which usually leads to a much worse problem (and a bigger odor issue) than the original one.

Is there a warranty on the repair work?

Sealed entry points are backed by a workmanship warranty — if a raccoon reopens the same spot, we return to fix it at no extra charge.

Do you serve businesses as well as homes?

Yes. We handle raccoon removal and exclusion for warehouses, restaurants, retail buildings, and multi-family properties in and around Butler County.

Are raccoons dangerous?

Raccoons can carry rabies and their droppings can contain roundworm eggs, so direct contact should always be avoided. Our technicians handle wildlife and cleanup safely so you don’t have to.

What if there’s a litter of kits in the attic?

We locate the den, confirm the kits are mobile, and use a one-way door timed so the mother can move them out on her own before we seal the entry point.
