Prevention checklist for Fruitland homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Install secure lids on trash cans and bring pet food inside at night; easy food access is the single biggest thing that keeps raccoons returning to a property.
  • Skunk Removal: Seal gaps under decks, sheds, and porches with buried hardware cloth extending at least 12 inches below grade -- a surface-level patch alone won't stop a skunk from digging under it.
  • Mosquito Control: Clean gutters regularly; clogged gutters that pool water are one of the most common mosquito breeding sites we find on a property.
  • Rat Control: Fix leaking outdoor faucets or irrigation lines; a reliable water source is as much of a draw as food.
  • Roach Control: Clean behind and under large appliances periodically -- refrigerators, stoves, and dishwashers trap crumbs and moisture that roaches rely on.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: Inspect secondhand furniture, especially mattresses and upholstered pieces, carefully before bringing them into the home.
