Prevention checklist for University Park homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Cap the chimney with a certified chimney cap; an open flue is one of the most common raccoon entry points we find.
  • Skunk Removal: Keep pet food and water bowls inside at night; outdoor pet food is one of the most reliable skunk attractants we see.
  • Mosquito Control: Clean gutters regularly; clogged gutters that pool water are one of the most common mosquito breeding sites we find on a property.
  • Rat Control: Keep outdoor trash in cans with tight-fitting lids, and don't leave bagged garbage out overnight.
  • Roach Control: Store food, including pet food, in sealed containers rather than the original packaging.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: Use a protective mattress and box spring encasement, which makes it much easier to spot an early infestation before it spreads.
