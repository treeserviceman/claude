Prevention checklist for Taneytown homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Seal gaps around dormers and where additions meet the original roofline -- these transition points are common weak spots on older homes.
  • Skunk Removal: Secure trash cans with tight-fitting lids and avoid leaving bagged garbage out overnight.
  • Mosquito Control: Add or maintain fish in any decorative pond; mosquitofish and many common pond fish eat larvae before they mature.
  • Rat Control: Trim vegetation and tree limbs away from the house; overgrown shrubs against the foundation give rats cover to approach unseen.
  • Roach Control: Check secondhand appliances and furniture carefully before bringing them inside; roaches (and their egg cases) travel this way more often than people expect.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: Use a protective mattress and box spring encasement, which makes it much easier to spot an early infestation before it spreads.
