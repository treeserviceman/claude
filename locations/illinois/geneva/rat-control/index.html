Our Rat Control process

We start with an inspection for entry points, droppings, gnaw marks, and grease trails along baseboards or beams, which tell us where rats are traveling. Exclusion work seals gaps with steel wool, hardware cloth, or metal flashing (never anything a rat can chew through), and we place tamper-resistant bait stations or snap traps based on the level of activity.

Signs you need rat control

Look for droppings (dark, rice-grain sized, often along walls or in cabinets), gnaw marks on wood or wiring, greasy rub marks along baseboards, and scratching sounds in walls or the attic at night.

Why Geneva homeowners call us

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if it's rats or mice in my Geneva home?

Rat droppings are noticeably larger (about the size of a raisin vs. a grain of rice for mice), and rats tend to be louder and bolder in their movement through walls and attics.

How long does rat control take to work?

Exclusion stops new entry immediately; clearing an existing population with traps or bait typically takes one to three weeks depending on how established the nest is.

Is rat bait safe around pets?

We use tamper-resistant, lockable bait stations specifically to keep pets and children away from the bait itself.

Can rats really fit through small gaps?

Yes -- an adult rat can squeeze through an opening about the size of a quarter, which is why sealing gaps is as important as trapping.
