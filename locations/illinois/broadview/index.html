Prevention checklist for Broadview homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Cap the chimney with a certified chimney cap; an open flue is one of the most common raccoon entry points we find.
  • Skunk Removal: If you see a skunk during the day, keep pets inside and stay back -- daytime activity can (though doesn't always) signal illness.
  • Mosquito Control: Keep grass and shrubs trimmed -- adult mosquitoes rest in shaded, overgrown vegetation during the day.
  • Rat Control: Store pet food and birdseed in sealed metal or thick plastic containers rather than the original bag.
  • Roach Control: Seal cracks around baseboards, cabinets, and where pipes enter walls with caulk to reduce available harborage.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: If you live in a multi-unit building and suspect an infestation, notify management promptly; shared walls and hallways can let bed bugs spread between units.
