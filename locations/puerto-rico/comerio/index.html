Prevention checklist for Comerio homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Trim tree limbs back at least 6-8 feet from the roofline -- raccoons use overhanging branches as a bridge onto the roof.
  • Skunk Removal: Secure trash cans with tight-fitting lids and avoid leaving bagged garbage out overnight.
  • Mosquito Control: Screen rain barrels with fine mesh if you use one for irrigation, since an open barrel is an ideal breeding site.
  • Rat Control: Address clutter in garages, sheds, and basements -- rats prefer nesting sites with cover, and stacked boxes or stored materials give them exactly that.
  • Roach Control: Seal cracks around baseboards, cabinets, and where pipes enter walls with caulk to reduce available harborage.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: When traveling, check hotel mattress seams and headboards, and keep luggage off the bed and floor when possible.
