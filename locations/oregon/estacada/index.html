Prevention checklist for Estacada homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Keep compost bins secured with a locking lid rather than an open pile, especially if it's within a few feet of the house.
  • Skunk Removal: Keep pet food and water bowls inside at night; outdoor pet food is one of the most reliable skunk attractants we see.
  • Mosquito Control: Clean gutters regularly; clogged gutters that pool water are one of the most common mosquito breeding sites we find on a property.
  • Rat Control: Fix leaking outdoor faucets or irrigation lines; a reliable water source is as much of a draw as food.
  • Roach Control: Check secondhand appliances and furniture carefully before bringing them inside; roaches (and their egg cases) travel this way more often than people expect.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: Reduce clutter near the bed -- fewer hiding spots make an early infestation easier to catch and treat before it establishes.
