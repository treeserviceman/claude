Prevention checklist for St. John homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Trim tree limbs back at least 6-8 feet from the roofline -- raccoons use overhanging branches as a bridge onto the roof.
  • Skunk Removal: Install motion-activated lights near common den sites (under decks, sheds); skunks generally avoid well-lit, high-traffic areas.
  • Mosquito Control: Clean gutters regularly; clogged gutters that pool water are one of the most common mosquito breeding sites we find on a property.
  • Rat Control: Fix leaking outdoor faucets or irrigation lines; a reliable water source is as much of a draw as food.
  • Roach Control: Fix leaking pipes and faucets promptly -- roaches need very little water to survive, and a small consistent leak can sustain a population on its own.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: Wash and dry travel clothing on high heat as soon as you get home, even if you don't suspect exposure.
