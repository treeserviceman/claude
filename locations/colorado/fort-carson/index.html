Prevention checklist for Fort Carson homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Cap the chimney with a certified chimney cap; an open flue is one of the most common raccoon entry points we find.
  • Skunk Removal: Keep pet food and water bowls inside at night; outdoor pet food is one of the most reliable skunk attractants we see.
  • Mosquito Control: Store tarps and covers so they don't pool rainwater in the folds; a bunched tarp is a surprisingly common source.
  • Rat Control: Seal gaps around pipes, vents, and utility lines where they enter the home -- steel wool or hardware cloth, not just caulk, which rats can chew through.
  • Roach Control: Fix leaking pipes and faucets promptly -- roaches need very little water to survive, and a small consistent leak can sustain a population on its own.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: When traveling, check hotel mattress seams and headboards, and keep luggage off the bed and floor when possible.
