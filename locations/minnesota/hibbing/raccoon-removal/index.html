Our Raccoon Removal process

We inspect the roofline, attic, and foundation for access points first, since sealing a home before confirming raccoons are out just traps them inside. Once we've verified the space is clear, we exclude and seal with materials sized to actually stop a raccoon -- not the thin patch jobs that get torn open again within a week.

Signs you need raccoon removal

Common signs include heavy thumping or rolling sounds in the ceiling at night, matted-down insulation, a strong ammonia smell from urine buildup, torn roof vents or soffits, and raccoon droppings (dark, tubular, often on a roof edge or in a corner of the attic) that should not be handled bare-handed.

Frequently asked questions

What attracts raccoons to homes in Hibbing?

Unsecured trash cans, pet food left outdoors overnight, and roof or soffit gaps that offer a dry, quiet den site are the biggest draws we see.

Can raccoons cause real damage, or is it just noise?

Beyond noise, raccoons compress and soil attic insulation, chew wiring and duct work, and their droppings can pose a health risk, so it's worth treating as more than a nuisance.

Do you handle raccoons in chimneys too?

Yes -- chimneys are one of the most common raccoon den sites we find in Hibbing, especially uncapped ones.

Is a single visit usually enough?

Trapping can take a few days depending on the animal's schedule, but most jobs wrap up with one exclusion and follow-up visit once the space is confirmed empty.
