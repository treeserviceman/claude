Prevention checklist for Chatfield homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Trim tree limbs back at least 6-8 feet from the roofline -- raccoons use overhanging branches as a bridge onto the roof.
  • Skunk Removal: Install motion-activated lights near common den sites (under decks, sheds); skunks generally avoid well-lit, high-traffic areas.
  • Mosquito Control: Keep grass and shrubs trimmed -- adult mosquitoes rest in shaded, overgrown vegetation during the day.
  • Rat Control: Trim vegetation and tree limbs away from the house; overgrown shrubs against the foundation give rats cover to approach unseen.
  • Roach Control: Clean behind and under large appliances periodically -- refrigerators, stoves, and dishwashers trap crumbs and moisture that roaches rely on.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: If you live in a multi-unit building and suspect an infestation, notify management promptly; shared walls and hallways can let bed bugs spread between units.
