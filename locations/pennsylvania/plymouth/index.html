Prevention checklist for Plymouth homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Keep compost bins secured with a locking lid rather than an open pile, especially if it's within a few feet of the house.
  • Skunk Removal: Keep pet food and water bowls inside at night; outdoor pet food is one of the most reliable skunk attractants we see.
  • Mosquito Control: Check for water pooling in tire ruts, low patches of yard, or under AC unit drip lines after it rains.
  • Rat Control: Store pet food and birdseed in sealed metal or thick plastic containers rather than the original bag.
  • Roach Control: Store food, including pet food, in sealed containers rather than the original packaging.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: When traveling, check hotel mattress seams and headboards, and keep luggage off the bed and floor when possible.
