Prevention checklist for Ripley homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Install secure lids on trash cans and bring pet food inside at night; easy food access is the single biggest thing that keeps raccoons returning to a property.
  • Skunk Removal: Seal gaps under decks, sheds, and porches with buried hardware cloth extending at least 12 inches below grade -- a surface-level patch alone won't stop a skunk from digging under it.
  • Mosquito Control: Screen rain barrels with fine mesh if you use one for irrigation, since an open barrel is an ideal breeding site.
  • Rat Control: Address clutter in garages, sheds, and basements -- rats prefer nesting sites with cover, and stacked boxes or stored materials give them exactly that.
  • Roach Control: Check secondhand appliances and furniture carefully before bringing them inside; roaches (and their egg cases) travel this way more often than people expect.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: Wash and dry travel clothing on high heat as soon as you get home, even if you don't suspect exposure.
