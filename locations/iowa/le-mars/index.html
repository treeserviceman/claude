Prevention checklist for Le Mars homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Install secure lids on trash cans and bring pet food inside at night; easy food access is the single biggest thing that keeps raccoons returning to a property.
  • Skunk Removal: Secure trash cans with tight-fitting lids and avoid leaving bagged garbage out overnight.
  • Mosquito Control: Empty anything holding water for more than a few days -- plant saucers, buckets, birdbaths, kiddie pools -- at least once a week.
  • Rat Control: Keep outdoor trash in cans with tight-fitting lids, and don't leave bagged garbage out overnight.
  • Roach Control: Fix leaking pipes and faucets promptly -- roaches need very little water to survive, and a small consistent leak can sustain a population on its own.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: If you live in a multi-unit building and suspect an infestation, notify management promptly; shared walls and hallways can let bed bugs spread between units.
