Prevention checklist for Winnsboro homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: If a raccoon has denned in the attic before, assume it (or another raccoon) will try the same spot again unless the original entry point was permanently sealed, not just blocked.
  • Skunk Removal: Seal gaps under decks, sheds, and porches with buried hardware cloth extending at least 12 inches below grade -- a surface-level patch alone won't stop a skunk from digging under it.
  • Mosquito Control: Check for water pooling in tire ruts, low patches of yard, or under AC unit drip lines after it rains.
  • Rat Control: Store pet food and birdseed in sealed metal or thick plastic containers rather than the original bag.
  • Roach Control: Seal cracks around baseboards, cabinets, and where pipes enter walls with caulk to reduce available harborage.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: Use a protective mattress and box spring encasement, which makes it much easier to spot an early infestation before it spreads.
