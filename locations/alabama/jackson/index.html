Prevention checklist for Jackson homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Check attic access points, roof valleys, and any gap where a utility line enters the house at least once a year, ideally before spring denning season.
  • Skunk Removal: Treat the lawn for grubs if digging holes are widespread -- skunks are foraging for a food source, and removing it makes the yard less attractive.
  • Mosquito Control: Store tarps and covers so they don't pool rainwater in the folds; a bunched tarp is a surprisingly common source.
  • Rat Control: Address clutter in garages, sheds, and basements -- rats prefer nesting sites with cover, and stacked boxes or stored materials give them exactly that.
  • Roach Control: Check secondhand appliances and furniture carefully before bringing them inside; roaches (and their egg cases) travel this way more often than people expect.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: Use a protective mattress and box spring encasement, which makes it much easier to spot an early infestation before it spreads.
