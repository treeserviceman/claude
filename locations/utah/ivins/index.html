Prevention checklist for Ivins homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Install secure lids on trash cans and bring pet food inside at night; easy food access is the single biggest thing that keeps raccoons returning to a property.
  • Skunk Removal: Treat the lawn for grubs if digging holes are widespread -- skunks are foraging for a food source, and removing it makes the yard less attractive.
  • Mosquito Control: Add or maintain fish in any decorative pond; mosquitofish and many common pond fish eat larvae before they mature.
  • Rat Control: Keep outdoor trash in cans with tight-fitting lids, and don't leave bagged garbage out overnight.
  • Roach Control: Store food, including pet food, in sealed containers rather than the original packaging.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: When traveling, check hotel mattress seams and headboards, and keep luggage off the bed and floor when possible.
