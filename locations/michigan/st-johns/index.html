Prevention checklist for St. Johns homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Install secure lids on trash cans and bring pet food inside at night; easy food access is the single biggest thing that keeps raccoons returning to a property.
  • Skunk Removal: Secure trash cans with tight-fitting lids and avoid leaving bagged garbage out overnight.
  • Mosquito Control: Store tarps and covers so they don't pool rainwater in the folds; a bunched tarp is a surprisingly common source.
  • Rat Control: Fix leaking outdoor faucets or irrigation lines; a reliable water source is as much of a draw as food.
  • Roach Control: Take trash out regularly and use a can with a tight-fitting lid, especially in the kitchen.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: Use a protective mattress and box spring encasement, which makes it much easier to spot an early infestation before it spreads.
