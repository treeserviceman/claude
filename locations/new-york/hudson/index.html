Prevention checklist for Hudson homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Cover soffit and gable vents with heavy-gauge hardware cloth, not standard window screen, which a raccoon can tear through in minutes.
  • Skunk Removal: Keep pet food and water bowls inside at night; outdoor pet food is one of the most reliable skunk attractants we see.
  • Mosquito Control: Add or maintain fish in any decorative pond; mosquitofish and many common pond fish eat larvae before they mature.
  • Rat Control: Trim vegetation and tree limbs away from the house; overgrown shrubs against the foundation give rats cover to approach unseen.
  • Roach Control: Store food, including pet food, in sealed containers rather than the original packaging.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: If you live in a multi-unit building and suspect an infestation, notify management promptly; shared walls and hallways can let bed bugs spread between units.
