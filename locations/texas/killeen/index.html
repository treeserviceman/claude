Prevention checklist for Killeen homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Trim tree limbs back at least 6-8 feet from the roofline -- raccoons use overhanging branches as a bridge onto the roof.
  • Skunk Removal: Keep pet food and water bowls inside at night; outdoor pet food is one of the most reliable skunk attractants we see.
  • Mosquito Control: Check for water pooling in tire ruts, low patches of yard, or under AC unit drip lines after it rains.
  • Rat Control: Address clutter in garages, sheds, and basements -- rats prefer nesting sites with cover, and stacked boxes or stored materials give them exactly that.
  • Roach Control: Take trash out regularly and use a can with a tight-fitting lid, especially in the kitchen.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: Inspect secondhand furniture, especially mattresses and upholstered pieces, carefully before bringing them into the home.
