Prevention checklist for Lochmoor Waterway Estates homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Check attic access points, roof valleys, and any gap where a utility line enters the house at least once a year, ideally before spring denning season.
  • Skunk Removal: If you see a skunk during the day, keep pets inside and stay back -- daytime activity can (though doesn't always) signal illness.
  • Mosquito Control: Empty anything holding water for more than a few days -- plant saucers, buckets, birdbaths, kiddie pools -- at least once a week.
  • Rat Control: Address clutter in garages, sheds, and basements -- rats prefer nesting sites with cover, and stacked boxes or stored materials give them exactly that.
  • Roach Control: Clean behind and under large appliances periodically -- refrigerators, stoves, and dishwashers trap crumbs and moisture that roaches rely on.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: If you live in a multi-unit building and suspect an infestation, notify management promptly; shared walls and hallways can let bed bugs spread between units.
