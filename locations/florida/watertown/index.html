Prevention checklist for Watertown homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Check attic access points, roof valleys, and any gap where a utility line enters the house at least once a year, ideally before spring denning season.
  • Skunk Removal: Seal gaps under decks, sheds, and porches with buried hardware cloth extending at least 12 inches below grade -- a surface-level patch alone won't stop a skunk from digging under it.
  • Mosquito Control: Empty anything holding water for more than a few days -- plant saucers, buckets, birdbaths, kiddie pools -- at least once a week.
  • Rat Control: Store pet food and birdseed in sealed metal or thick plastic containers rather than the original bag.
  • Roach Control: Store food, including pet food, in sealed containers rather than the original packaging.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: Inspect secondhand furniture, especially mattresses and upholstered pieces, carefully before bringing them into the home.
