Prevention checklist for Maumee homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Cap the chimney with a certified chimney cap; an open flue is one of the most common raccoon entry points we find.
  • Skunk Removal: Treat the lawn for grubs if digging holes are widespread -- skunks are foraging for a food source, and removing it makes the yard less attractive.
  • Mosquito Control: Screen rain barrels with fine mesh if you use one for irrigation, since an open barrel is an ideal breeding site.
  • Rat Control: Seal gaps around pipes, vents, and utility lines where they enter the home -- steel wool or hardware cloth, not just caulk, which rats can chew through.
  • Roach Control: Check secondhand appliances and furniture carefully before bringing them inside; roaches (and their egg cases) travel this way more often than people expect.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: If you live in a multi-unit building and suspect an infestation, notify management promptly; shared walls and hallways can let bed bugs spread between units.
