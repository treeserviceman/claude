Prevention checklist for Liberty Lake homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Install secure lids on trash cans and bring pet food inside at night; easy food access is the single biggest thing that keeps raccoons returning to a property.
  • Skunk Removal: Treat the lawn for grubs if digging holes are widespread -- skunks are foraging for a food source, and removing it makes the yard less attractive.
  • Mosquito Control: Check for water pooling in tire ruts, low patches of yard, or under AC unit drip lines after it rains.
  • Rat Control: Trim vegetation and tree limbs away from the house; overgrown shrubs against the foundation give rats cover to approach unseen.
  • Roach Control: Check secondhand appliances and furniture carefully before bringing them inside; roaches (and their egg cases) travel this way more often than people expect.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: Reduce clutter near the bed -- fewer hiding spots make an early infestation easier to catch and treat before it establishes.
