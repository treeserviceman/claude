Prevention checklist for Clearlake Riviera homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Keep compost bins secured with a locking lid rather than an open pile, especially if it's within a few feet of the house.
  • Skunk Removal: Seal gaps under decks, sheds, and porches with buried hardware cloth extending at least 12 inches below grade -- a surface-level patch alone won't stop a skunk from digging under it.
  • Mosquito Control: Empty anything holding water for more than a few days -- plant saucers, buckets, birdbaths, kiddie pools -- at least once a week.
  • Rat Control: Fix leaking outdoor faucets or irrigation lines; a reliable water source is as much of a draw as food.
  • Roach Control: Check secondhand appliances and furniture carefully before bringing them inside; roaches (and their egg cases) travel this way more often than people expect.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: If you live in a multi-unit building and suspect an infestation, notify management promptly; shared walls and hallways can let bed bugs spread between units.
