Prevention checklist for Allendale homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Cover soffit and gable vents with heavy-gauge hardware cloth, not standard window screen, which a raccoon can tear through in minutes.
  • Skunk Removal: Install motion-activated lights near common den sites (under decks, sheds); skunks generally avoid well-lit, high-traffic areas.
  • Mosquito Control: Empty anything holding water for more than a few days -- plant saucers, buckets, birdbaths, kiddie pools -- at least once a week.
  • Rat Control: Address clutter in garages, sheds, and basements -- rats prefer nesting sites with cover, and stacked boxes or stored materials give them exactly that.
  • Roach Control: Store food, including pet food, in sealed containers rather than the original packaging.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: Wash and dry travel clothing on high heat as soon as you get home, even if you don't suspect exposure.
