Prevention checklist for Socastee homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: If a raccoon has denned in the attic before, assume it (or another raccoon) will try the same spot again unless the original entry point was permanently sealed, not just blocked.
  • Skunk Removal: Install motion-activated lights near common den sites (under decks, sheds); skunks generally avoid well-lit, high-traffic areas.
  • Mosquito Control: Keep grass and shrubs trimmed -- adult mosquitoes rest in shaded, overgrown vegetation during the day.
  • Rat Control: Address clutter in garages, sheds, and basements -- rats prefer nesting sites with cover, and stacked boxes or stored materials give them exactly that.
  • Roach Control: Seal cracks around baseboards, cabinets, and where pipes enter walls with caulk to reduce available harborage.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: Reduce clutter near the bed -- fewer hiding spots make an early infestation easier to catch and treat before it establishes.
