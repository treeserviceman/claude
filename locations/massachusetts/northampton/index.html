Prevention checklist for Northampton homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Check attic access points, roof valleys, and any gap where a utility line enters the house at least once a year, ideally before spring denning season.
  • Skunk Removal: Install motion-activated lights near common den sites (under decks, sheds); skunks generally avoid well-lit, high-traffic areas.
  • Mosquito Control: Screen rain barrels with fine mesh if you use one for irrigation, since an open barrel is an ideal breeding site.
  • Rat Control: Store pet food and birdseed in sealed metal or thick plastic containers rather than the original bag.
  • Roach Control: Clean behind and under large appliances periodically -- refrigerators, stoves, and dishwashers trap crumbs and moisture that roaches rely on.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: Reduce clutter near the bed -- fewer hiding spots make an early infestation easier to catch and treat before it establishes.
