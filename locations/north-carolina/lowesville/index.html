Prevention checklist for Lowesville homes

One quick habit for each pest we cover here -- the full guide for each service has a longer checklist.

  • Raccoon Removal: Trim tree limbs back at least 6-8 feet from the roofline -- raccoons use overhanging branches as a bridge onto the roof.
  • Skunk Removal: If you see a skunk during the day, keep pets inside and stay back -- daytime activity can (though doesn't always) signal illness.
  • Mosquito Control: Clean gutters regularly; clogged gutters that pool water are one of the most common mosquito breeding sites we find on a property.
  • Rat Control: Store pet food and birdseed in sealed metal or thick plastic containers rather than the original bag.
  • Roach Control: Take trash out regularly and use a can with a tight-fitting lid, especially in the kitchen.
  • Bed Bug Treatment: Use a protective mattress and box spring encasement, which makes it much easier to spot an early infestation before it spreads.
